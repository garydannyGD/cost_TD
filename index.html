📖 Learn while you defend

Classify costs, distinguish cost units and centres, interpret cost behaviour, calculate prime and production cost, use high–low estimation and flex a budget. The last challenge applies your knowledge to a new business.

🎮 How to play

  1. Solve two challenges before each wave. Correct answers unlock building stars; retries are welcome.
  2. Choose a tower, then tap an empty plot. Tap a built tower to upgrade or sell it.
  3. Send the wave! Towers attack automatically. Use your hero power when the blobs get close.

Learning marks record first answers only. Village health, wave retries and speed never reduce learning marks. Questions are always untimed.

📖 Pocket reference

Prime cost = direct materials + direct labour + direct expenses.

Production cost = prime cost + production overhead.

High–low variable cost = change in total cost ÷ change in activity. Choose highest and lowest activity.

Total cost = fixed cost + variable cost per unit × activity.
